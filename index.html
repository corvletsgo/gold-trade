How a trade works

Simple, and the metal gets checked before anything ships. No surprises on either side.

1

Pick an item

Find what you want in the catalog. Note its gold price (grams of pure gold) or, on items $50 and under, its silver price (troy ounces).

2

Send a trade offer

Use the offer form below. Name the item, which metal you're paying with, and the form it's in — Goldbacks, a coin, a bar in assay, or silver rounds. Pick how you want the item: shipped in the US, shipped to South Korea, local pickup, or local drop-off.

3

Metal is verified first

Goldbacks are checked by UV light and serial number; coins and bars are tested. The price in metal locks at spot on the day your metal is received. Your item ships only after the metal checks out — that protects both of us.

4

Get it your way

US shipping, South Korea shipping on collectibles and bare tools/parts, local pickup, or local drop-off by the seller. Local trades are checked in person at the meet.

5

Cash still works

Every item with an eBay link can be bought for cash there, exactly like always. This page is just the gold lane — same seller, same items.

The rules, plain and honest

Gold accepted

  • Goldbacks (checked by UV + serial)
  • US and Canadian bullion coins — Eagles, Maples
  • Small bars in assay
  • Priced at spot on the day your gold is received — gold moves daily

Silver accepted — items $50 and under

  • Silver rounds
  • 90% US coins (pre-1965)
  • Silver bars
  • Also priced at spot on the day it's received

Shipping & batteries

  • EGO lithium batteries are ground-shipping only — they cannot ship to South Korea (no air freight, period)
  • Korea shipping is for collectibles and bare tools/parts only
  • Bulky items are local pickup/drop-off only, as marked

No returns

All trades and sales are final — the seller's standing policy on eBay too. Items are described as tested and working where it says so, and the metal is verified before your item ships, so both sides are covered up front.

How paying in gold works

Five steps, in order. The metal always moves first and gets verified — then your item ships.

1

Send the order form

Fill out the order form below and send it. It lands straight in our inbox — no account to make, nothing to pay yet.

2

Get your exact amount by email

You’ll get an email from corvletsgo@gmail.com with the exact amount of metal to send and where to send it. Your price in metal locks at spot on the day your metal is received. Don’t send any metal before that email.

3

Send the metal

PAX Gold (PAXG) goes by crypto transfer. Goldbacks, fractional gold coins or bars, and silver go by mail — always with tracking.

4

We verify it

Goldbacks are checked by UV light and serial number, coins and bars are tested, and PAXG is confirmed on-chain. Your item ships only after the metal checks out — that protects both of us.

5

Your item ships with tracking

We print the shipping label from the address you gave us on the form and send you the tracking. EGO batteries ship ground-only inside the US.
